* **Tech Stack:** CNC Mill, CNC Lathe, Tool Offset Calibration, Tolerance Stackup Analysis, SolidWorks, Fusion 360.
* **Quantifiable Impact:** Successfully executed tight-tolerance stackups in direct collaboration with senior machinists, producing a fully functional, high-precision assembly.

### Project 4: Baja SAE Gas Tank Mount Structural Reinforcement
* **Context & Role:** Chassis Fabrication Team Member | Notre Dame Baja SAE
* **Problem Statement:** Designed and fabricated a critical structural reinforcement for the off-road vehicle gas tank mount to meet strict SAE competition safety standards.
* **Tech Stack:** Waterjet Cutting, Steel & Aluminum Joint Preparation, Power Tools, SAE Standards.
* **Quantifiable Impact:** Passed technical inspection, ensuring gas tank security under extreme dynamic off-road racing conditions.

### Project 5: Marmon Holdings Conductive Heating PVC Pipe Fitting Tool
